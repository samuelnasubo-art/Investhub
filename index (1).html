A signal is Strong when all three digit rules hold: every losing digit (0, 1, 2 for Over 2; 7, 8, 9 for Under 7; the five digits of the other kind for THE GUARDIAN) is below 10% over the last 1000 ticks; none of them is rising faster than the rise limit over the last 100 ticks; and at least 3 of the winning digits are at 10.5% or more. Two rules met is Average, one is Weak. The Win % column is the last-100-tick win rate, shown for information. THE GUARDIAN has its own rules: the three most appearing digits must all be winning-side digits at 10.5% or more, and the five losing-side digits must each be below 10.3% with at least 3 of them below 10%. The rise limit does not apply to THE GUARDIAN.

Bots. PROFIT TRACKER (Over/Under): Over 2 waits for its trigger digit (the least appearing of 1 and 2 over the last 1000 ticks), then an entry digit, then trades until take profit or stop loss. Under 7 waits for its trigger digit (the least appearing of 7 and 8), then an entry digit, then trades. The entry digits follow the PROFIT TRACKER entry setting: Opposite side (Over 0 to 2, Under 7 to 9) or Winning side (Over 3 to 8, Under 1 to 6). THE GUARDIAN (Even/Odd): it waits for the least appearing digit on the winning side to be hit, then for 2 (or 3, see the THE GUARDIAN entry setting) losing-side digits in a row, then for one winning-side confirmation digit, and then trades the winning side until take profit or stop loss. SIZZLERS (Matches): a signal appears when one digit is the most touched over the last 1000 ticks (at least the min share, default 11%), also leads the last 100 ticks, and is gaining share by the min rise (default 2 points). The bot is made for that digit: once the cursor touches it, it buys Matches on it on every tick until take profit or stop loss, with a fixed stake (no martingale and no cooldown). It reads past frequency and trend only. Digits are independent, so this does not predict the next digit, and a Matches win pays roughly 9 times the stake but only comes about 1 in 10 ticks. THE AUTO TRADE AI (Over 4 / Under 5): it counts which side the cursor touched most in the last 800 ticks (digits 5 to 9 give Over 4, digits 0 to 4 give Under 5; a signal needs at least the min side share, default 53%). The bot enters a trade on that side each time 3 consecutive losing-side digits appear (digits 0 to 4 when it trades Over 4, digits 5 to 9 when it trades Under 5), for the next 200 ticks (800 read plus 200 traded makes 1000), then reads the latest 800 ticks again and picks the side again. It uses the multiplier after a loss but no loss cooldown. The side that attracted the cursor in the past does not make it more likely next, since each digit is independent. After a loss PROFIT TRACKER and THE GUARDIAN take a cooldown (default 20 seconds), then start their entry steps again with the multiplied stake. On the 1-second volatilities and the Jump indices, a bot waits 2 ticks after its entry before trading (or uses 2-tick contracts, see the 1s & Jump setting).
